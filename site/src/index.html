The approved design decision your coding agent reads.

A lightweight, open-source workflow for design and product teams. Every feature moves through six short docs in your repo, passes a human decision gate, and ends as a spec that developers and coding agents can build from.

$ npx discovery-to-spec init
Try it in your browser View on GitHub

MIT licensed. Zero dependencies. No telemetry.

01Installation

$npm i -D discovery-to-spec
$npx d2s init
$npx discovery-to-spec init
$node path/to/discovery-to-spec/bin/d2s.js init

Requires Node.js 18 or later, for whoever runs the tool. Add --lang he for Hebrew templates.

Upgrading to 0.6? Update the package, then run d2s init --force. It refreshes the templates you haven’t changed and keeps the ones you have, with a list of the new sections to add to them. Explorations that passed Frame or Spec before the upgrade show the new sections as warnings, not errors, so CI keeps passing. Designers get the new Figma plugin from the release, and the new AI assistant below.

02Workflow

  1. /d2s-frameframe the problem: who it hurts, the outcome you want, the appetite and the no-gos
  2. /d2s-researchturn interviews, tickets and data into insights with evidence
  3. /d2s-optionslay out 2 to 4 genuinely different directions, each linked to Figma
  4. /d2s-decidechoose one and record why. Only people approve: product, design and engineering, each by name.
  5. /d2s-specevery screen, state and edge case, with acceptance criteria you can test
  6. /d2s-handoffone generated doc for development. Rejected directions stay out.

Slash commands for Claude Code and Cursor. The same flow runs from the terminal with d2s new, d2s next and d2s status.

    04An AI assistant for the thinking
    
  
    Development got fast. The thinking before it is now the slow part. The d2s assistant works in the chat you already use, Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, and gets you to “what should we build” faster, without skipping the thinking.
    
  
    
    
    Frames the problem
    Asks the right questions, pushes back when a request arrives as a solution, and asks where it lives in your product today.
    
    
    Makes sense of research
    Turns raw interview notes, tickets and data into insights with evidence and a confidence level. Quotes stay word for word, and names come out.
    
    
    Lays out real directions
    Two to four genuinely different directions, each with what it changes in the product. Then the decision and the spec.
    
  
    
  
    One discovery session. Bring a new request, and the assistant takes it through the problem, the evidence and the directions in one conversation. It ends with one document: Paste from AI fills Frame, Research and Options in turn, and the gate checks each. Options stays open for design to add the frames, and the decision stays with the team.
    
  
    It never invents research, quotes or Figma links, and never approves for anyone. Paste your design/context.md and it works from your product, users, design principles and design system. Each stage ends with a document that has your team’s headings, and Paste from AI in d2s fills the form from it.

    08Questions
    
  
    
    
    Do designers need a terminal?
    No. Try the Designer view above: every stage is a form with a Next stage button, and it writes the same Markdown files the CLI does. The Figma plugin brings the same forms into Figma, and anyone can also edit the files on github.com.
    
    
    Does it replace OpenSpec?
    No, it comes before it. OpenSpec starts once you know the change you want. discovery-to-spec helps you get there, and drafts an OpenSpec proposal when you do.
    
    
    Do we need a paid Figma plan?
    No. Figma links are plain links in the docs. Agents can read the frames through the Figma MCP server if you use it, but nothing depends on it.
    
    
    Can an AI agent approve a decision?
    No. The skills tell agents never to fill in who approved, and the gate wants one line per person with a name, role and date. It rejects placeholders like “TBD” and the names of AI tools, and it can check each approval against a GitHub review. Agents help you write; people decide.
    
    
    Can we change the templates?
    Yes. They live in design/templates in your repo. Every heading is required unless it’s marked optional, and the check follows your version.
    
    
    Which languages are supported?
    Templates come in English and Hebrew, right to left included. The CLI’s own messages are in English. More languages are welcome as contributions.
