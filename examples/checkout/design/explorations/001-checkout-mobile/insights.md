# Mobile checkout drop-off: insights

> Stage 2 of 6: research and synthesis. Only what has evidence behind it. Never invent quotes or numbers.

## Sources
Fictional example. Six short customer interviews, 120 support tickets from the last quarter, and funnel data from analytics.

## Key insights
1. **The form is too long for a small screen.** Four of six interviewees said they "give up and finish on the computer". Confidence: high.
2. **Card errors are unclear.** About a quarter of the support tickets describe "the payment didn't go through" with no explanation. Confidence: medium.
3. **Cards can't be saved.** Returning customers re-enter their details on every order. Confidence: medium.

## Opportunities
- Shorten the form on mobile.
- Explain payment errors in plain language, with what to do next.
- Let returning customers pay without re-entering their details.

## Still unclear (optional)
Whether customers are comfortable saving a card with us.
