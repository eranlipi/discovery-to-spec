# Mobile checkout drop-off

> Stage 1 of 6: frame. One page at most. Describe the problem, not the solution.

## Problem
<!-- What isn't working today, and for whom? If the request arrived as a solution ("add a button"), ask which problem it is meant to solve. -->
Fictional example. About 40% of users who reach checkout on mobile don't complete it. On desktop the rate is much lower, so we suspect the mobile experience rather than pricing.

## Who it matters to
<!-- Which users are affected, and in what situation or moment. -->
Returning customers who order from their phone, usually during a short break or on the go.

## Desired outcome
<!-- The result you want, ideally something you can observe or measure. E.g. "fewer support tickets about changing an address". -->
More completed mobile orders, and fewer support tickets saying "the payment didn't go through".

## Appetite
<!-- How much time is this worth? E.g. "two weeks of a small team". This is a decision, not an estimate. -->
Three weeks of a small team: a designer, a product manager and a developer.

## No-gos
<!-- Clear limits: what is out of scope for this piece of work. -->
We don't switch payment providers and we don't change prices or shipping.

## Open questions (optional)
<!-- What is still unknown, and who can answer it. -->
Do users drop off before entering card details, or after?
